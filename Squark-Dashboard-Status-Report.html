3. Phase 1 — work completed

Phase 1 delivered 22 modules. These are grouped below by function.

3.1 Access and identity

  • Login with secure session handling
  • Nine roles with a four-level permission structure
  • Organisation passcode required for sensitive changes
  • Audit log of all significant actions

3.2 Project delivery

  • Projects across nine patent-analysis types, each with a standard task list
  • Project ID (PID) system with multiple rounds and a full ledger
  • Tasks and subtasks with staffing
  • Timesheets with backdating rules
  • Patent and client records, access-restricted

3.3 People operations

  • Attendance with punch in and out, and work-from-home
  • Attendance regularisation requests routed to HR
  • Leave and compensatory off with balances and approvals
  • Expenses from submission to reimbursement
  • Staff directory with restricted access to personal details
  • Appraisals, company announcements and policy library

3.4 Reporting

  • Performance charts and contribution summary
  • Team capacity planner
  • Reports with export to CSV and PDF
  • Daily digest at 10:00 pm
  • Individual and team calendars

4. Phase 2 — work completed

4.1 Client and patent management

Client codes, archiving of inactive clients, tagging of patents to projects after creation, and a client ledger showing work done and its value. Retired patent identifiers continue to resolve after a client code is changed.

4.2 Team spaces and Business Development pipeline

Previously all work in the system had to be a client project, leaving HR and Business Development work with nowhere to sit. Team spaces were created for non-delivery work, kept separate from client delivery records. A Business Development pipeline was added covering deals, stages, activity history, forecasting and win/loss reporting.

4.4 Latest work — 23 August 2026

The following was completed on 23 August and is awaiting deployment to the live system.

  • Patent ID to patent number mapping. Staff assigned to a project can now see the actual patent numbers for that project. Previously only the Super Admin could, which meant analysts could not identify the patents they were working on. Access is granted by project membership. Client identity remains restricted, and every access is recorded.
  • Feedback module. Any employee may record feedback about a colleague. It is visible to the author, HR and the employee's reporting manager.
  • Client ledger. Client records now hold contact details, country, industry, account manager and a billing rate, allowing the value of work to be calculated automatically from hours logged.
  • Project manager selection. Managers and Senior Consultants can now be named as manager of their own projects.
