Phase 1What it built — closed 12 August

Twenty-two modules replacing spreadsheets and WhatsApp with one system the whole firm signs into.

Access & identity

Cookie/JWT login with real sessions. Nine roles across a four-layer permission model — role presets, per-user grants, per-user denials, project membership — so access is described once and enforced everywhere. An organisation passcode as a second factor on changes that cannot be walked back. A full audit log.

Delivery

Projects across nine patent-analysis types, each with a technology domain and a standard task list created on the spot. The PID system minting SQ_26_27_nnn with rounds and a ledger. Tasks and subtasks with staffing. Timesheets with backdating windows — free under a month, approved to three, refused beyond. Patents and clients, held confidentially.

People operations

Attendance with punch in/out, WFH as a work mode, and regularisation routed to HR. Leave and comp-off with balances. Expenses through to reimbursement. A people directory with a hard PII boundary. Appraisals, a company feed, and a policy library.

Insight

Performance charts and a contribution heatmap. A capacity board answering who is free and when. Reports with CSV and PDF export. A 10pm daily digest. Personal and team calendars.

Phase 2Four parts, opened 12 August

Part 1 — Clients and patents · 13 Aug

Typed client codes with a suggested default. Archive and Remove as genuinely different actions — archive is reversible, Remove is refused while any patent or project still points at the client. Patents taggable after creation. The client ledger as its own screen, recomputed on every read rather than stored, with a Super-Admin override that records who stated what and when. formerHandles, so an ID quoted from an old email still resolves after a rename.

Part 2 — Team spaces and the BD pipeline · 13–16 Aug

Every piece of work in the system was a client project — enforced, not conventional. HR and BD work had nowhere to live but a "General" project in the delivery list. Team spaces became their own destination, deliberately off the delivery spine: no PID, no client, absent from both ledgers. BD got a full pipeline, where losing a deal requires a reason because the aggregate answer to "why do we lose" is the most useful thing it produces. Timesheet.teamId now keeps three states apart that were one ambiguous blank.

Part 4 — The latest batch · 23 Aug · PR #100, not yet merged

Self as project manager. "May run a project" and "may mint the PID" had been folded into one flag, and only Admin and Super Admin can mint — so every Manager and Senior Consultant was told to hand their own matter to an Admin. Now separate.

Feedback. Anyone may write about a colleague; the author, HR and the subject's reporting manager may read it — the subject cannot, as you specified.

Client ledger. A client was a code and a name, so nothing knew a rate and money could never be derived. It now carries contact, country, industry, account manager and a rate, so value is hours × rate and moves with the work.

Patent ID ↔ number. Three tiers with project membership as the grant. A member resolves the real numbers for their own matters, in both directions, without seeing the client. Every reveal audited by count, never by number.
