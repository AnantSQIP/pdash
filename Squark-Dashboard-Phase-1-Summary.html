Squark Dashboard — Phase 1

Summary of everything built and running to date · August 2026
The Squark Dashboard is one place for the whole team to run their day — the patent work we deliver, the hours we spend on it, and the people side (attendance, leave, expenses and reviews). Everything listed below is built, tested and in use. Each person only sees the parts their role allows, so the screen stays relevant to their job.

1Logging In & Access

  • Secure login with your own password; sessions time out when idle
  • 8 roles — Super Admin, Admin, Manager, Senior Consultant, Consultant, Senior Research Associate, HR and Employee
  • Menus and pages appear only if your role allows them
  • Sensitive changes (people, roles) ask for an extra org passcode

2Home Screen

  • Changes to suit your role — a manager and an analyst see different things
  • Your tasks, your projects and your key numbers at a glance
  • Punch In / Punch Out button right on the home page
  • Notifications with a click-through to the item

3Projects

  • Every project gets a PID such as SQ_26_27_001; a returning client keeps the same PID across new rounds of work
  • 9 project types — Infringement, Novelty, Invalidity, FTO, Landscape, Monetization, Reverse Engineering, Risk & Strategy, General
  • Picking a type creates that type's standard task list automatically
  • A technology domain too (Medical, Automobile, Source Code, Cloud/Server, Advertisement) — your own can be added
  • View work as a list, a Kanban board or a Gantt chart
  • Files, discussion, issues and activity history on each project
  • Complete, close and reopen a project when work finishes

4Patents & Clients

  • Confidential area — Super Admin only
  • Real patent numbers are hidden behind short codes like Pat_MLK_001
  • The real number is shown only after entering the passcode
  • Client codes and patent documents stored together
  • Projects link to patents, so the client is always correct

5Tasks

  • Tasks and sub-tasks under each project
  • Assign people with a role — PM, Reviewer or Analyst — and estimated hours
  • Deadlines, priority, status and progress on every task
  • "My Tasks" gathers your work across all projects in one list

6Timesheets

  • Log hours against a project PID and then the task
  • Colour calendar shows each day at a glance — green, amber or red by hours logged
  • Mark your own time billable or non-billable
  • Backdating rules: under a month is free, 1–3 months needs approval, over 3 months is closed
  • Reminders if time has not been filled in

7Attendance

  • Punch in and out; location is recorded with the punch
  • Under 4 hours is counted as a half day automatically
  • Work from home goes through a request and HR approval
  • Missed or wrong punches can be sent to HR to correct
  • Monthly view, one-year history and CSV export

8Leaves & Comp-off

  • Apply for leave as a full day or half day
  • Goes to the right approver, with balance shown before you apply
  • Comp-off for weekend and holiday work — claim, HR approves, balance credited
  • Balance card shows clearly what was credited and what was used
  • Company holidays are built in

9Expenses

  • Submit an expense with the receipt attached
  • Goes for approval, then is marked reimbursed
  • Admins and HR are notified of new claims
  • You see your own; approvers see the queue

10Team Capacity

  • Board showing who is free and who is loaded, day by day
  • Leave, holidays and work-from-home shown on the same view
  • Look back over the past 30 days, or ahead at what is coming
  • Filter by project team to focus on one matter
  • Extend a deadline or reassign work when someone is suddenly out

11Performance

  • Your own scores — completion rate, on-time rate, hours and activity
  • Organisation view for leadership: project progress, top contributors, rankings
  • Charts and a contribution heat map over time
  • Every number has a short note explaining how it is worked out

12Reports & Daily Digest

  • Project and task reports with filters
  • Export to CSV or PDF
  • Daily digest at 10 pm — the day's projects, hours and deadlines
  • Digest is Super Admin only, with a full detail screen

13Calendar

  • Your deadlines, meetings, leave and holidays in one calendar
  • Team Calendar to see everyone's month together
  • Scrollable timeline — one month back, three months ahead

14People & Profiles

  • Directory of the team with photos, roles and designations
  • Personal page for each person with their work and roles
  • Personal details (address, date of birth, next of kin) visible only to Admin and HR
  • New joiners complete their profile at first login

15Company

  • Company announcements feed
  • HR policy library, with a record of who has read what
  • Org chart showing reporting lines
  • Birthdays and work anniversaries

16Appraisals

  • Review cycles run by HR
  • Self review, then manager review, then you acknowledge
  • Goals recorded against each review
  • Recognition and rewards can be given to team members

17Discussion & Alerts

  • Discussion tab on every project
  • @mention a project member and they are notified
  • Attach files and images to messages and comments
  • Search across projects, tasks and people from any screen

18Admin Tools

  • Manage roles and permissions from one matrix screen
  • PID ledger — every PID issued, used or reserved
  • Audit log of who changed what and when
  • Add and manage team members
Squark Dashboard · Phase 1 summary · prepared August 2026. All items listed are live in the system. Access to any screen depends on the role assigned to each user.
