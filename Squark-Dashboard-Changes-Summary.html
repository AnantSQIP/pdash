Squark Dashboard — Summary of Changes

All updates delivered to date · consolidated and de-duplicated
Everything below is listed once, grouped by module. Items marked NEW are features that did not exist before; FIX means something was behaving incorrectly and has been corrected.
NEW = new feature FIX = corrected behaviour

ATimesheets

  1. Changed to a calendar view instead of a list.
  2. Bigger tiles, with hours shown in a larger, readable font.
  3. Days are clickable — clicking a day opens its detail panel.
  4. The day panel shows every entry filled that day (PID, task, hours, billable, notes), with Assign PID, Delete and “Log for this day”.
  5. Separate logs table removed — logs now live inside the day panel.
  6. Backfill moved into a collapsed “Backfill an older month” toggle.
  7. FIX Calendar updates in real time after you log or delete — no refresh needed.
  8. NEW Maximum 16 hours can be logged against one day.
  9. FIX A half-day leave keeps its 4-hour target (it used to wrongly clear the whole day).

BAttendance

  1. FIX The “24 hours” glitch — shifts are capped at 16 hours.
  2. Shifts left open are closed automatically at 11:59 pm.
  3. Punch-in popup on first login of the day.
  4. NEW “Punch in (Work from home)” — punches you in and raises a WFH request; its status (pending / approved / declined) is shown in the popup.
  5. FIX Present is never shown without a punch. Filling a timesheet alone no longer marks a day present.
  6. FIX A punch after midnight now counts for the correct day.
  7. Area column — taken from where the person actually punched in.
  8. Full column names: Punch In / Punch In Location / Punch Out / Punch Out Location.
  9. Date picker to view punch locations for any day in the past year.
  10. CSV export for any date range you choose.
  11. NEW Admin view of who is working from home vs office — today, past week, 2 weeks or 30 days.
  12. FIX Regularization requests no longer error on submit.
  13. NEW “Worked past midnight” regularization option.
  14. Admins and Super Admins are now notified of comp-off and regularization requests.

CLeave & Comp-off

  1. NEW Half-day leave — morning or afternoon.
  2. Four clear request options: full-day leave, half-day leave, comp-off credit, comp-off avail.
  3. Comp-off claim can be half day or full day.
  4. FIX A half-day comp-off now earns half a day. It used to earn a full day.
  5. Approved comp-off shows in attendance as Present / Half-day, marked “comp-off”.
  6. Approved comp-off becomes a required working day on the timesheet (8h / 4h).
  7. Pending comp-off shows an amber asterisk; approved shows a “CO” marker.
  8. FIX “Comp Off” is always visible as a leave type, even at zero balance.
  9. Balance card is bigger and clearer — “3 out of 4 remaining” instead of “3 / 4 left”.
  10. You cannot avail more comp-off than you have earned.
  11. NEW Pending leave, WFH and comp-off requests are visible in every calendar.

DTeam Capacity & Roles

  1. FIX The assign error when adding a PM without hours.
  2. One person can hold several roles on the same task (PM + Reviewer + Analyst).
  3. Every assignment has its own hours and deadline.
  4. Assign directly from the capacity board: person → project → task → role, hours and deadline.
  5. FIX Today is no longer shown as “Present” before anyone has punched in.

EProjects

  1. NEW “Save as template” now sticks — a saved type appears for everyone next time.
  2. Project type shown as a tag.
  3. Card layout reordered: Name → PID → Phase → Type.
  4. List view now shows the PID.
  5. FIX Date pickers — the deadline could previously only pick an earlier date.
  6. NEW A project cannot be marked complete while tasks are still open.
  7. IDEA phase removed.
  8. NEW On completion the system asks for: client delivery date & time, working hours (auto-filled from timesheets, editable) and actual hours (typed manually).
  9. Delivery date and hours appear on the project, PID ledger, reports and digest.

FPID

  1. NEW A PID is never assigned automatically — it must be generated on purpose.
  2. PID text is bigger and bolder.
  3. NEW Re-initialize a completed or closed project from the PID ledger, keeping the same PID.
  4. Ledger detail shows the delivery date and hours.

GCalendar

  1. All calendars now start on Monday.
  2. FIX New colours — every type is clearly different. Green now only means “completed”.
  3. Pending and approved leave / WFH / comp-off are shown in all calendars.
  4. FIX Multi-day leave now shows on every day, not just the first.
  5. Team Calendar rewritten — frozen date row and member column, with avatars, names and designations.
  6. NEW Team Calendar has a Company row for holidays and org-wide events.
  7. Upcoming panel limited to this and next month, and can be collapsed.
  8. NEW Celebrations in Upcoming — birthdays and wedding anniversaries.
  9. Profile captures marital status and wedding anniversary.

HDaily Digest  (Super Admin only)

  1. NEW Now a proper module under Administration, visible to Super Admins only.
  2. Every number is clickable and opens the rows behind it.
  3. Projects show PID, type, dates, delivery, hours, progress, PM and full team — all linked.
  4. Tasks show project, status, deadline, hours and each assignee’s own hours and date.
  5. Hours logged per person, expandable to every individual entry.
  6. NEW “Coming up — next 5 working days”, day by day (weekends and holidays skipped).
  7. Send time is admin-editable, with a “Send now” button.
  8. The digest notification links straight to the module.
  9. NEW Full CSV export covering every section (it was 2 columns).

IReports

  1. Activity digest removed — it now lives in the Daily Digest module.
  2. NEW Project Type column.
  3. NEW Search by PID, project or client.
  4. NEW Expanding a project spotlights it — highlighted row, bordered panel, everything else dimmed.
  5. The expanded panel shows type, client, all dates, delivery, working / actual / logged / estimated hours, tasks, patents, team and per-task staffing.
  6. NEW Download a single project as CSV.
  7. Full CSV export — 30 columns plus a task-and-staffing section (it was 7 columns).

JHome

  1. NEW “Attendance & comp-off requests” card for HR / Admin / Super Admin — approve or reject from the homepage.

KColours

  1. Timesheet calendar colours match the attendance calendar — the same meaning is the same colour.
  2. PID Ledger colours match the project phases (Working = blue, Completed = green; they were effectively swapped).
